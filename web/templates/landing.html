CHANAKYA UNIVERSITY
Built for Chanakya University

Everything a CU-connect network needs, without the noise.

Each feature maps to a data structure you can defend in the viva.

Two feeds, one engine

A max-heap merges your connections' posts by time. Explore reads the global stream from the end.

Trie search

Prefix search that costs the prefix length, not the user count.

Events & RSVP

Calendar dict plus a min-heap for whatever is next.

Clubs & groups

Public groups open to all, private ones gated by owner approval.

Roles & moderation

Admin-only role changes, a role log, report queues and post review — enforced in the engine, not the template.

How it works

Three moves to find your people.

01 / SIGN UP

Make a profile

Pick a username, tell people what you study. Passwords are salted and hashed.

02 / CONNECT

Build your graph

Send requests, accept the ones that matter, and watch second-degree suggestions appear.

03 / PARTICIPATE

Post, RSVP, join

Share updates, react to your connections, RSVP to events and join communities.
